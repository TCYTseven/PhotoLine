Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

This policy explains what PhotoCards (the "app") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: we collect the minimum needed to run a multiplayer game, we never sell data, we don't track you across other apps, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

Who we are

PhotoCards is published by the PhotoCards team. Contact us through the support page.

What we collect

What we do not collect

  • We never access your camera or photo library. All game photos come from a curated library we host.
  • No email address, phone number, password or real name is required.
  • No precise or approximate location.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How data is used

Only to provide the game: matching you with the players in your room, showing prompts and photos, keeping score, and enforcing reports and blocks. We do not sell or rent personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Your display name, your submitted photos (once revealed) and your score are visible to the other players in the same room. Data is processed by Supabase, our hosting provider, on our behalf. Photos in the starter library are served by Lorem Picsum / Unsplash under their licenses. We disclose data only if required by law.

Retention

  • Game rooms and everything in them are deleted automatically within hours of the game ending.
  • Your guest account and display name stay until you delete them.
  • Reports are kept as long as needed to review them.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and data from inside the app: Settings → Delete account & data. This permanently removes your guest account, display name, gameplay data, reports and blocks from our servers.
  • Change your display name at any time on the home screen or in Settings.
  • Block or report players from any photo's report menu during a game.
  • Depending on where you live you may have additional rights (access, correction, portability, objection). Contact us via the support page and we will help.

Children

PhotoCards is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with row-level access controls so players can only read data for rooms they are in.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you inside the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: see the support page.
