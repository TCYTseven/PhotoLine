How it works

1. Make a room

One player creates a room and shares a six letter code. Friends join on their own phones. No accounts, no sign-up.

2. Read the prompt

“My face when someone says trust me”. One player judges each round; everyone else plays.

3. Pick a photo

Each player gets 16 random photos from the curated PhotoCards library. Pick the one that fits best before the timer ends.

4. Judge & score

Photos are revealed anonymously. The judge picks a winner, or everyone votes in Vote mode. First to the target score wins.

Modes

  • Classic – one judge per round.
  • Vote – everyone votes, most votes wins.
  • Rapid Fire – 15 second timers for chaos.

Built with privacy in mind

PhotoCards never reads your camera roll. Photos come from a curated library, players are guests with a display name only, and you can delete everything from inside the app at any time. Read the privacy policy.
