Help & support

Get the app

PhotoCards is an iOS app. Once it is live on the App Store this page will link to it. Until then, it is distributed through TestFlight.

Frequently asked

How many players do I need?

Three or more, on separate phones. The host creates a room and shares the six letter code.

I closed the app during a game. Did I lose my spot?

No. Reopen the app and you are put straight back into the room. You can also re-enter the code from the Join screen.

A room code says "not found"

Codes only work while the room is open. Rooms close a few hours after they end, or when the host leaves an empty lobby.

Can I use my own photos?

Not yet. PhotoCards never reads your camera roll; all photos come from a curated library.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete account & data. Everything tied to your guest account is removed from our servers immediately.

Report a problem or a player

Inside a game, open any photo and tap the flag to report it, the player, or the prompt. You can block the player at the same time. Reports are reviewed by the team.
