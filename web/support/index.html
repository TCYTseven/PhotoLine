Help & support

Get the app

PhotoCards is an iOS app. Once it is live on the App Store this page will link to it. Until then, it is distributed through TestFlight.

Frequently asked

How many players do I need?

Three or more, on separate phones. The host creates a room and shares the six letter code.

I closed the app during a game. Did I lose my spot?

No. Reopen the app and you are put straight back into the room. You can also re-enter the code from the Join screen.

A room code says "not found"

Codes only work while the room is open. Rooms close a few hours after they end, or when the host leaves an empty lobby.

Can I use my own photos?

Not yet. PhotoCards never reads your camera roll; all photos come from a curated library.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete account & data. Everything tied to your guest account is removed from our servers immediately. There is no password or email to recover, so this can't be undone.

Report a problem or a player

Inside a game you can report a photo (tap the flag on an enlarged photo), a player, or a custom prompt, and block a player so they can't join rooms you host. Blocked players can be unblocked in Settings → Blocked players. Reports are reviewed by the team within 24 hours; objectionable content is removed and the player responsible can be banned.

Contact us

For bugs, questions and feature ideas, open an issue on our public tracker. Please don't post personal information there; your guest account can be deleted from inside the app without contacting us.
